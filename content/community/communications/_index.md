OSGeo is an active and vibrant community, with many ways to connect!

OSGeo offers an inclusive safe space for everyone. Please review our [code of conduct](</code_of_conduct/>).

### Discourse Forum

The primary communication channel is OSGeo Discourse Forum:

  * [OSGeo Discourse Forum](<https://discourse.osgeo.org/>)
  * The forum provides [categories](<https://discourse.osgeo.org/categories>) for a wide range of projects, [committees](<https://discourse.osgeo.org/c/committees/44>) and [initiatives](<https://discourse.osgeo.org/c/initiatives/41>).
  * Sign-in with OSGeo UserID, GitHub, or a Discourse Account

Please sign in and introduce yourself **[here](<https://discourse.osgeo.org/c/introductions/>),** we look forward to meeting you.

### Mailing lists

#### Discussion List

To get involved in the Open Source Geospatial Foundation, we invite you to[ Subscribe](<https://lists.osgeo.org/mailman/listinfo/discuss>) and participate in the discussion e-mail list.

**Using the Discuss List**

  * Only subscribers can send email to the [discuss@osgeo.org](<mailto:discuss@osgeo.org>)
  * Email questions, ideas and suggestions for discussion.
  * Everyone can review the [archive.](<https://lists.osgeo.org/pipermail/discuss>)
  * Please avoid emailing others in the list directly, so that everyone can benefit from the discussion.
  * Before you post a question to the list, please search the mailing list archive to see if answers can be found. Newcomers may find this to be a valuable resource, and should feel free to ask questions if they cannot find satisfactory answers in the archive.

#### Announcements List

If you want to know about what is going on in the Open Source Geospatial Foundation, you are encouraged to:

  * [Subscribe](<https://lists.osgeo.org/mailman/listinfo/announce>) to the announcements e-mail list.
  * Review the [archive](<https://lists.osgeo.org/pipermail/announce/>) for past announcements.

### Chat

Join the conversation! OSGeo uses [Matrix](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/Matrix>) as a decentralized, real-time chat protocol, that is bridged to different chat systems.

  * [matrix]: [#osgeo:matrix.org](<https://matrix.to/#/#general:osgeo.org>) ([preview](<https://view.matrix.org/room/!ziPhIMTeslyTkMWFeD:osgeo.org/?anchor=$kDH-It90Z0FTFSAc1VJv7QWcuY1j3wgWHuay-j2k0aU&offset=30>))
  * Libera.chat web client: [#osgeo](<https://web.libera.chat/#osgeo>), or use your own [IRC](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/IRC>) client.
  * Slack: [osgeo.slack.com](<https://app.slack.com/client/T07RKPVGV>)

Chat is an opportunity for the OSGeo Community hang out and conduct the occasional interactive meeting. Drop in to informally get to know people and what they’re working on.
