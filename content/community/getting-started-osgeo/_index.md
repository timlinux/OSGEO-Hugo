We are happy to have you on board. On this page, you will learn **how to become part of the OSGeo Community**. For more information on who we are and what we do see [About OSGeo](</about/>).

## Join the Conversation

We look forward to meeting you – join the conversation on one of our [forums, mailing lists or social networks.](</community/communications/>)

OSGeo offers an inclusive safe space for everyone. Please review our [code of conduct](</code_of_conduct/>).

## Local Chapters

Starting out with any new technology can be intimidating. OSGeo offers worldwide access to “local chapter” groups to help you get started:

  * Local Chapters provide OSGeo members an opportunity to connect, network and share.
  * Regional Chapters hold events, offer support, training and explore advocacy opportunities in their area.
  * Local chapters can also form around a specific language or industry supporting a geographical diverse community.
  * Each Chapter is different with a mix of GIS professionals, academic champions, software developers and service providers eager to share their stories and help you start your open source journey.

See the list of [local chapters](</local-chapters/>) to find a group in your area.

## Explore the Wiki

We have a [Wiki](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/>) where we document **what’s happening in the community**.

  * To add your own activities you will need an [OSGeo User ID](<https://id.osgeo.org/ldap/create>). Click the ‘[log in](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/w/index.php?title=Special:UserLogin&returnto=Main+Page>)‘ button on the upper right hand corner of the Wiki and follow the instructions.
  * For more information about using a Wiki, see the [Wiki Help](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/Help:Contents>).
