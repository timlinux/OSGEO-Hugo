## Logo and branding material usage

These are the official branding material and logo for the Open Source Geospatial Foundation (OSGeo).

We have established a [Branding Guide](<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OSGeo/osgeo/master/marketing/branding/styleguide-osgeo.pdf> "OSGeo Branding Style Guide") (PDF – 3.95 MB), which provides guidelines for logo usage, colours, typeface and branding elements to ensure that we have a consistent visual identity. Please respect our [trademark guidelines](<https://www.osgeo.org/legal-information/>) when using these visual assets.

## Branding Material Downloads

The [OSGeo Branding and Style Guide](<https://github.com/OSGeo/osgeo/blob/master/marketing/branding/styleguide-osgeo.pdf>) provides instructions for use:

  * OSGeo branding materials are [available in github](<https://github.com/OSGeo/osgeo/tree/master/marketing/branding>).
  * OSGeo Foundation logo is [available](<https://github.com/OSGeo/osgeo/tree/master/marketing/branding/logo>) in a range of web friendly (png, svg) and publishing (eps, ai) formats.
  * OSGeo [printing collateral](<https://github.com/OSGeo/osgeo/tree/master/marketing/print>) has been provided by the foundation and projects and translated by our local chapter volunteers.
  * Collateral [templates](<https://github.com/OSGeo/osgeo/tree/master/marketing/collateral>) are provided, for effective use please follow the customization instructions to include your own [background imagery.](<https://github.com/OSGeo/osgeo/tree/master/marketing/photos>)

Please contact the marketing committee if you require assistance or clarification on the use of OSGeo branding materials.

## Logo History

The origional OSGeo logo created by Aaron Koning’s as part of a community contest and subsequently altered to allow for easier printing, but the initial clear design concept remains.

In 2017 a website and rebranding contract was under taken by [Get Interactive](<https://www.getinteractive.nl/>). The firm lead the community through a [rebranding exercise](</foundation-news/website-and-branding-may-update/>), updating our core compass design elements for our ten year anniversary.
