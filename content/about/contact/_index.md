Thank you for your interest in contacting OSGeo. Note that depending on your area of inquiry, there are a number of different ways to connect.

## Technical questions

Remember: all project or software related questions (technical and non-technical) should be addressed directly to the appropriate project-specific forum or mailing list.

  * List of OSGeo project mailing lists: <https://lists.osgeo.org/mailman/listinfo>
  * List of OSGeo forum categories: <https://discourse.osgeo.org/categories>

## General questions and comments

General questions and comments should be sent to OSGeo’s Discuss mailing list or General Forum.

  * Subscribe at: <https://lists.osgeo.org/mailman/listinfo/discuss>
  * General: <https://discourse.osgeo.org/c/general/4>

## Contact OSGeo Board of Directors

If you would like to discuss with OSGeo’s Board of Directors, send your message to the board mailing list.

Please subscribe at <https://lists.osgeo.org/mailman/listinfo/board>

## Info Address

Email: [info@osgeo.org](<mailto:info@osgeo.org>)

  * The info address is not monitored regularly, and forwards only to a few recipients and is therefore not recommended.
  * Please use one of the above mailing lists to guarantee a proper response to your query.
