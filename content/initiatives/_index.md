Initiatives are a great opportunity to see our community in action – often gathering together our members, open source projects, and partners.

  * [Free and Open Source Software For Geospatial](</initiatives/foss4g/>) (FOSS4G) – our annual global under the care of the [conference committee](</about/committees/conference-committee/>), and a series of regional open source outreach events hosted by our [local chapters](</archive-templates/local-chapters/>).
  * [FOSS4G Travel Grant Program](</initiatives/foss4g-travel-grant-program/>) – With your assistance OSGeo provides a travel grant program to facilitate accessibility and diversity at our global and regional FOSS4G events.
  * [GeoForAll](</initiatives/geo-for-all/>) – making geospatial education and opportunities accessible to all
  * [GSOC](</initiatives/gsoc/>) – initiative taking advantage of the [Google Summer of Code](<https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/>) program to provide students with first hand open source experience
  * [Local Chapters](</local-chapters/>) – regional OSGeo groups promoting open source around the world and close to home
  * OSGeo Live – an [easy to use distribution](</projects/osgeo-live/>) of open source geospatial software for outreach and evaluation
  * [Open GeoScience](</initiatives/open-geoscience/>) – applying the principles of open source to make scientific research, data and results transparent and accessible for science and society – under the care of the open geoscience committee
  * [UN initiative ](</initiatives/un-initiative/>)support the UN migration to Open Source Geospatial software solutions – under the care of the UN Committee
