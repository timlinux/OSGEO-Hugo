As a part of [Google Summer of Code](<https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/>), student participants are paired with a mentor from the participating organizations, gaining exposure to real-world software development and techniques. In turn, the participating organizations are able to identify and bring in new developers who implement new features and hopefully continue to contribute to open source even after the program is over. Most importantly, more code is created and released for the use and benefit of all.

**Google Summer of Code participation**

Browse the [Wiki pages for GSoC 2023](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/Google_Summer_of_Code_2023>) for important recommendations on how to become a [successful student](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/Google_Summer_of_Code_Recommendations_for_Students>) or [a mentor](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/Google_Summer_of_Code_2021_Administrative>). The OSGeo Projects’ GSoC 2023 Ideas page is available [here](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/Google_Summer_of_Code_2023_Ideas>).

**OSGeo Commitment to Students**

The Open Source Geospatial Foundation has been participating in this activity as a way of introducing students to our vibrant open source community and exploring interesting innovative ideas.
