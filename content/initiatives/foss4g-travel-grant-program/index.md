# Supporting the Travel Grant Program

The travel grant program is funded through:

  * Ongoing crowdfunding campaign
  * Collections at regional FOSS4G events
  * Sponsor donations, [recognized](<http://osgeo.org/sponsors/>) as part of OSGeo [sponsorship program](</about/how-to-become-a-sponsor/>)
  * Annual commitment from the OSGeo board

# About the Travel Grant Program

The FOSS4G Travel Grant Programme was established in 2017 by the OSGeo Board to facilitate accessibility and diversity at our global and regional FOSS4G events.

The travel grant program is generously managed by the Conference Committee, in collaboration with representatives from each FOSS4G event:

  * Regional FOSS4G organising teams are encouraged to apply for TGP support, applications are welcome on the Conference Committee [mailing list](<http://osgeo-org.1560.x6.nabble.com/OSGeo-Conference-Committee-f3721662.html>).

**Resources:**

  * Guidance on running a TGP can be found in the [TGP Cookbook](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/FOSS4G_Travel_Grant_Programme_Cookbook>)
  * The [2017 TGP](<https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/FOSS4G_Travel_Grant_Programme_2017>), including [feedback from the 2017 grant recipients](<https://knowwhereconsulting.co.uk/blog/tales-from-boston-the-foss4g-tgp/>)
